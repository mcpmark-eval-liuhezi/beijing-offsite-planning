# Beijing Offsite Planning

This repository is the central planning hub for our team's offsite in Beijing.

## Purpose
- Collect and record venue research (hotels near the Forbidden City with measured travel times from Beijing South Railway Station)
- Track offsite logistics (arrival by high-speed rail, day plans around the Forbidden City)
- Draft the offsite agenda (maintained by Copilot from the venue research findings)

## Planning constraints
- Most of the team arrives by high-speed rail at Beijing South Railway Station
- Daytime activities are centered around the Forbidden City
- Venue must be an easy walk from the Forbidden City and a sane drive from the station

- `issues/` — venue research shortlist, decisions, and follow-up planning live in GitHub issues
